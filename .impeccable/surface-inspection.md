# Blaster inspection extension

Mode: Operate. Local extension of the approved workbench and range, using the existing models and visual system.

The user confirmed free 1:1 controller rotation after centering the right-hand, left-handle-forward grip. The separate inspection area has blaster selection, freeze/resume, recenter, zoom, and no timed round. Browser and physical testing remain with the user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the physical controller a stand-in for a blaster, so its orientation reveals every side of the artist's model.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the blue studio workspace, orange primary action, Barlow labels, mono readings, native controls, and both themes.

STORY: Open inspection, connect live motion, choose a blaster, center the sideways grip, then rotate freely. Freeze a pose to examine it; recenter after changing grip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A broad, uncluttered model stage with tracking status and center/freeze actions above it, plus a compact blaster/zoom/mapping/grip column. Mobile stacks the controls below the model.

FORM: An additional expandable workbench tool, inheriting the target range's composition. No new visual world or concept roll; the user specified the interaction and approved free rotation.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
