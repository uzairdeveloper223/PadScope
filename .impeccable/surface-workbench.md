# Controller workbench

Mode: Operate. Desktop-first single-page workbench. Primary task: test the connected controller with live input beside a PS5-style model; reach calibration and hardware controls on the same page.

## Grounded directions

Ordered cultural sources: console-accessibility manuals; competitive match scorecards; racing-event programs; Japanese repair-parts catalogs; precision console-accessory packaging; tournament brackets; technical-museum collections. Candidate five was assigned and approved. The signature is the artist-made physical object responding in lockstep with paired measurements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A controller is an object under test. The first screen shows it and its actual inputs together, without a marketing hero.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink-blue shell, ice-blue workspace, white measurement surfaces, warm-orange actions, green left-stick data and orange right-stick data. Barlow gives compact, printed-label clarity; measurement numerals use a dedicated mono.

STORY: Connect or press a button, identify the chipset, watch paired raw/corrected stick readings and articulated model parts, then calibrate or test outputs. Empty and preview states remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact ink-blue header. Title and device strip. Large left 3D stage and right analog plots/triggers. A full-width button strip closes the workspace. Hardware connection is top-right; calibration follows directly below.

FORM: Precision console-accessory packaging, grounded candidate five; seed:977ab1e529b82e7a. The source lends type, palette, density, and object-led live motion, with standard web controls and navigation.
