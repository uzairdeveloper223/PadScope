The mirror prepares the artist's existing geometry into named, disconnected controls and bakes transforms; the original geometry, normals, UVs, and materials are preserved. PadScope adds runtime control pivots, press highlighting, material/lighting adjustments, LED-color visualization, and live-input animation. Trigger hinges, smooth stick tilt, and fading stick-cap direction arrows follow the behavior of the mirrored DualSense Studio reference; shoulder buttons depress into the housing.
