How these measurements work

Hands-off results use five uninterrupted seconds of browser observations. P95 is the radius below which 95% of observations fall; jitter measures movement around the average position. The 5% reference is a PadScope comparison aid, not a manufacturer tolerance.

Stick sweeps record peak radius in 24 directions, ignoring movement inside radius 0.5. Circularity describes how the reported outline differs from a unit circle; square or shaped outputs can be normal. Trigger values describe reported input, not physical force or travel.

The warm-up checks that stick axes actually respond. Browser, driver, controller profiles, and firmware can filter input before it reaches PadScope. Focus loss pauses capture; incomplete hands-off windows restart. The check does not measure latency or certify hardware health. Full method and research sources.

How do I connect?

Use a data-capable USB cable or pair over Bluetooth. Press any controller button while this page is focused. Buttons and sticks work through the Gamepad API. In desktop Chrome or Edge, select Connect hardware and choose your controller to enable compatible touch, motion, lightbar, and HID rumble reports.

Connection instructions adapt to your operating system and browser.

Opening HID may require permission for /dev/hidraw*. See the Linux setup in the README.

What works on my controller?

PS5 DualSense, DualShock 4, and compatible controllers work for buttons and sticks. Touch, motion, vibration, and lights depend on the features your controller and browser expose. Connect hardware for supported USB and Bluetooth features.

Adaptive-trigger effects and microphone controls are not included. The PS button may be intercepted by your operating system.

What does calibration change?

Stick centers and radial deadzone affect the calibrated readings and model in PadScope. Motion zero samples the gyroscope bias and captures your resting pose. Stick centers and gyro bias are saved locally; the model starts centered each connection. These settings do not change controller firmware. Raw input remains available.
